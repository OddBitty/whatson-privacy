What's On Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

What's On is a community calendar app for Root, made by OddBitty. This page explains what information the app keeps, why it keeps it, and who can see it.

What the app keeps

When you use What's On in a community, the app saves:

  • Events: the title, description, image, times, time zone and categories, plus the Root user ID of the person who created the event.
  • RSVPs: your Root user ID, your answer (for example going or not going) and when you answered.
  • Invites: who was invited to an event, who invited them, and when.
  • Comments and reactions: what you write on an event, any emoji reactions, and when.
  • Scheduling polls: the times you mark as free, and who was invited to the poll.
  • Community settings: things like the community's time zone, time format, category colours and which roles can create events.
  • Admin activity log: when an event is changed, the app records what happened, who did it (their Root user ID and display name), the event title and the time. Only the newest 100 entries are kept.

What the app does not keep

  • Member names and profile pictures are loaded from Root when they are needed. The app does not save them, except for the display name in the admin activity log.
  • The app does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • The app does not send your information to any service outside Root.

Images

When you add a photo to an event, your device saves a new copy of it before upload. This removes hidden photo details, such as where the photo was taken. Animated GIFs are uploaded exactly as they are, so they keep any details saved in the file.

Where your information is stored

Everything the app keeps is stored in the database that Root provides for the app. Root's own privacy policy covers how Root stores and protects that data.

Your browser or device also remembers a few display choices, such as whether you have seen the welcome screen, which event list you last viewed, and the notifications shown in the app. These stay on your device.

Who can see it

  • Members of the community can see public events, who is going, and the comments on them.
  • Private events are only shown to the person who created them and the people they invite.
  • Community admins can see the admin activity log.
  • When someone creates a public event, the app posts a short message about it in the community's default channel. Private events are never posted.
  • When someone creates a poll, they can choose to post a short message about it in the community's default channel.

Notifications

The app sends some notifications through Root. Root shows them in your notifications, or as a push notification on your device when you are offline.

  • A reminder before an event starts, if you are going or created it.
  • A new poll, if the person who created it chooses to notify people.

Removing your information

  • You can change your RSVP until the event ends.
  • You can delete your own comments. Admins can delete comments too.
  • When an event is deleted, its RSVPs, invites and comments are deleted with it.
  • Community admins can clear the admin activity log.

If you would like to know what the app holds about you, or ask us to remove it, email us at the address below.
